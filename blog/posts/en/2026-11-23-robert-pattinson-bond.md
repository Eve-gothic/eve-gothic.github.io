Full confession: back in 2008, when the whole world split into Team Edward and Team Jacob, I was on Team "that pale boy looks like he wants to run off set". Eighteen years later, the same pale boy has been handed the most perfectly pressed tuxedo in cinema history. On Monday 16 November, Amazon MGM Studios officially announced that Robert Pattinson will be the new James Bond. A week on, the internet still can't stop talking about it, and neither can I.

## Number seven in a tux

London-born Pattinson is 40 and only the seventh actor in the official series, after Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Daniel Craig. He'll star in the 26th 007 film, directed by Denis Villeneuve (*Dune*, *Arrival*), written by *Peaky Blinders* creator Steven Knight and produced by Amy Pascal and David Heyman. According to the BBC, production is expected in 2027 with a release in 2028; there's no title or exact date yet. Seven years will separate *No Time To Die* (2021) from the new film, the longest gap in the franchise's history.

In his statement, Pattinson called being entrusted with 007 "the honour of a lifetime" and said calling it a dream come true is "an understatement". Villeneuve said Robert has "the timeless soul of 007" and brings a spirit that is completely new. The producers praised his "self-deprecating wit" and his ability to keep reinventing himself. Which is exactly what I love about him.

## From Cedric to Batman

Quick recap. First Cedric Diggory in the Harry Potter films, then Edward Cullen in *The Twilight Saga*, the role that made him hugely famous and hugely mocked. Instead of cashing in, he spent years picking strange, brave, often niche films, until he put on the cape in *The Batman* in 2022. And 2026 is simply his year: Zendaya's fiancé in *The Drama*, Antinous, one of Penelope's suitors, in the summer hit *The Odyssey*, and Chris Hansen in the thriller *Primetime*. In December he's Scytale in *Dune: Part Three*, with Villeneuve again. Clearly these two get each other.

Other names reported around the screen tests included Josh O'Connor, Callum Turner and Jack Lowden. But the studio went with a star everyone knows, one who has proved he can do both blockbusters and auteur cinema.

## Bond in the age of Amazon

There's a less romantic side too. This is the first Bond since Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson handed creative control to Amazon MGM in 2025 (Amazon bought MGM in 2021 for $8.5bn). The Hollywood Reporter says Jeff Bezos himself helped choose the new 007. A spy for the Crown picked partly by a rocket billionaire: if that isn't villain material, I don't know what is. I'll leave it to the writers.

And a little jab for the gentlemen who keep insisting Bond must be a "real man": your new agent started out as a sparkly vampire who brooded about his feelings. Masculinity, now with eyeliner. Delicious.

## The man, not the gossip

Privately, Pattinson has been with singer and actor Suki Waterhouse for years. Suki announced her pregnancy herself, on stage at Corona Capital festival in Mexico in November 2023, and their daughter arrived in 2024. That's what's officially known and that's enough; the rest I leave to the tabloids. I like men who laugh at themselves in interviews and wear fame lightly, and Pattinson has done that for two decades.

My prediction? The most gothic Bond ever. Dark eyes, a hint of unease, an ironic smile over a martini. Shaken, not stirred, and a little bit haunted. I'm already waiting.
