For creators

Your own fan app. Like this one.

A site your fans install on their phone like an app — with games starring you, comics, your gallery, videos and a blog, in seven languages. No app stores. It’s yours, not the algorithm’s.

Ask about your app → See what it can do ↓

I

What you get

  • ▣

    An app, without the app store

    Fans tap “Install” on Android or “Add to Home Screen” on iPhone. Your icon sits right next to Instagram — and every visit still counts as traffic to your site.

  • ✊︎

    Games starring you

    Short mobile games with your own character, outfits and colours. They save progress and keep working offline.

  • ✎

    Comics

    Your stories told in panels, with a reader built for phones: scroll, zoom, read.

  • ◐

    Gallery and video

    Photo shoots and short clips, presented like a magazine spread instead of lost in a feed.

  • ✦

    Blog

    Posts that are truly yours, with an RSS feed and pages Google can find. Nothing disappears after 24 hours.

  • ⁂

    Seven languages

    English, Polish, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Japanese and Chinese — so fans abroad feel at home.

  • ♪

    Notifications

    Fans who install the app can get a ping whenever you publish a new post, comic or shoot.

  • ◆

    Spots for your sponsors

    Give the brands you already work with something new: a banner, their product inside a game or comic, a discount code as a game reward. Always clearly labelled “Partner” or “Ad”.

  • ▤

    Reports for brands

    Simple numbers to show a sponsor: views, link clicks and game starts.

III

How it works

  1. I

    You send your photos and brand

    Photos or illustrations, your colours, the brands you work with, and what you’d like your fans to play and read.

  2. II

    We build your app

    The site, games with your character, comics and sponsor spots — set up and tested on real phones.

  3. III

    You share the link

    Put it in your bio, watch fans install it, and get a simple report to show your sponsors.

Good to know

  • Games and content a fan has already opened keep working offline.
  • Notifications work on Android. On iPhone they need iOS 16.4 or newer, and the app has to be added to the Home Screen first.
  • We only use photos and likenesses you own or have the rights to, and every sponsored item is clearly marked.
  • Eve is an AI character. Your app is built around you.
